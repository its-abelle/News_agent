# News Agent

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Writers and editors at Deluxe Studios discovering emerging-technology news and producing a sourced first draft. Their readers are Kenyan SME owners, brand builders, vibe coders and tech practitioners.

## Product purpose

Bring the existing Python/LangGraph editorial workflow into a local browser workspace. A writer discovers recent developments, chooses or revises a specific question, returns to saved work, and edits an article or follows a reporting brief when evidence is insufficient. Publication remains with the editor.

## Positioning

A source-led editorial assistant that keeps the reader's question and supporting evidence alongside the work.

## Brand personality

Dark, focused studio. The application name is News Agent. Deluxe Studios remains the editorial organisation.

## Design principles

- Keep the editorial decision visible: one selected pitch and one approved question.
- Make sources and reporting gaps inspectable, including unsuccessful research.
- Preserve work across browser and terminal sessions using the existing checkpoints.
- Distinguish live reporting, fictional sample material and the editor's own changes.
- Make article editing and downloads straightforward; publication stays outside the application.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Implementation targets include keyboard-operable native controls, explicit labels, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support. No additional accessibility requirements or external visual references were supplied.
